Axon Data Chatbot for Managers

While on contract at EY, I started building a chatbot for managerial-level personnel so they can talk to Axon data directly and retrieve what they need quickly, instead of waiting on analysts or digging through reports.

The goal is simple: ask a question, get the data. It grew out of my day-to-day work with audit datasets and my interest in LLM-based assistants.
